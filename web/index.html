starboy diy

every little guy rolled his own eyes · tap one to reroll it · hover a sleepy one to wake him up

starboy diy is a tiny wearable pet i built by hand. he clips onto your belt loop and acts like he's actually alive.

he feels the world through an accelerometer, a temperature sensor and a microphone. make a loud noise and he gets anxious. take him somewhere cold and he shivers. shake him and he gets dizzy, then mad at you. ignore him long enough and he dozes off and dreams.

every star rolls his own eyes the first time he turns on. there are 400 looks, 100 colorways across 4 eye shapes, each with its own rarity. once he rolls, those eyes are his forever.

he runs completely on his own. no app, no wifi, no account. just a thumb-sized esp32 board, a round 1.28" screen and a little battery, packed into a 20.5mm chrome shell i designed and 3d printed myself.

and he's fully open source. the cad, firmware and parts list are all on github, so you can build your own for about $97. it's an unofficial fan build inspired by creature's starboy, not something i sell.
